"My gremlin isn't following the cursor when I drag and drop"
- It the config.txt change the "FOLLOW_RADIUS" setting to a smaller number (150.0 > 50.0, etc)
- or if you want the gremlin to not follow your cursor to much make the number bigger (150.0 > 300.0, etc)

"My gremlin has this border and doesn't animate when dragging"
- It's probably your window's appearance and performance settings. Navigate through "adjust the appearance and performance of Windows" and check "Animate controls and elements inside windows"
